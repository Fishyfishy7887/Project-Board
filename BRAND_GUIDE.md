# Brand Guide

## Promise
Practical AI tools that help regular people make money, solve problems, and get more done.

## Headline
Use AI Better. Hustle Smarter.

## Brand line
No AI degree needed. Pick a prompt, add your details, and put the answer to work.

## Personality
Practical, confident, straightforward, helpful, approachable, motivating, affordable, working-person friendly.

## Visual system
Use nearly black backgrounds, warm off-white body copy, amber/gold accents, large readable headings, high-contrast CTAs, subtle gradients, and clean digital-product cards. Avoid robot clutter, cheesy money graphics, crypto styling, and guru aesthetics.
