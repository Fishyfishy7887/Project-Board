# Squarespace Implementation Guide

## Remove from existing template
Remove generic hero copy, sample products, fake testimonials, fake ratings, stock partner logos, countdown timers, and any payment forms that are not connected to the owner's approved checkout.

## Build sections in this order
1. Announcement bar: `New: Find your next realistic side hustle for only $4.99.`
2. Header: KING HUSTLE PLAN; Home, Shop, About, FAQ, Contact; CTA to the Side-Hustle Finder checkout.
3. Hero: headline, supporting copy, two CTAs, and product cover visual from `public/assets/side-hustle-finder-cover.svg`.
4. Problem/solution two-column section.
5. Featured Side-Hustle Finder product section with $4.99 price and checkout URL placeholder.
6. Four-step How It Works.
7. Before/after example transformation.
8. Who It Is For cards.
9. Coming Soon product cards.
10. Starter Kit upsell.
11. Trust statements.
12. FAQ accordion.
13. Email signup placeholder; connect only to owner's email platform.
14. Footer links and legal placeholders.

## Required owner inserts
- Real Side-Hustle Finder checkout URL.
- Real Starter Kit checkout URL.
- Real contact email.
- Final legal, privacy, terms, refund, and license language after review.
- Email platform embed/action URL if using signup.
